# Juliana · Financial Baseline

**SPEC_VERSION:** `2026-10-07.1`

## Condition

Load this module whenever Juliana is asked to execute, review, validate, complete or close a **Financial Baseline**, including the Financial Baseline inside the Lazaro Phase Zero.

## Objective

Build a reliable, reconciled and current financial snapshot that is sufficient for strategic and operational decision-making.

The baseline must allow:

- Lazaro to define realistic financial targets and assumptions;
- Laura to operationalize tasks, deadlines, documents, reminders and follow-up;
- Juliana to recommend actions that improve resilience, cash flow, savings capacity and net worth.

The Financial Baseline is not complete merely because a spreadsheet, report or dashboard exists. It is complete only when the financial picture is reliable enough to support decisions.

## Questions the baseline must answer

1. How much money and gross assets exist today?
2. What is the current net worth?
3. How much immediate liquidity is available?
4. What income exists and how stable is it?
5. What is the real monthly cost of living?
6. Which expenses are fixed, variable and extraordinary?
7. Which debts, installments, financing, taxes and financial commitments are active?
8. Which recurring bills and future obligations need provisioning?
9. What is the real monthly cash flow?
10. What is the current savings rate?
11. How much can be saved sustainably?
12. How much can be invested sustainably?
13. What is the financial runway in months?
14. Which assets are liquid and which are illiquid?
15. What material financial risks exist?
16. Which expenses can be reduced, eliminated or renegotiated?
17. Which material obligations exist over the next 90 days?

## Required separation

Always keep these categories distinct:

- income;
- expenses;
- cash flow;
- debts and financial commitments;
- gross assets;
- net worth;
- liquidity;
- investments;
- reserves;
- taxes and expected obligations.

Do not double count the same asset, balance, transfer or liability across categories.

## Crypto boundary

Cryptoassets may be included in consolidated net worth and liquidity classification when relevant.

Technical decisions about crypto allocation, buying, selling, custody or strategy belong to Primo Rico.

## Source and evidence rules

Prefer real, current data from available financial sources, including when applicable:

- Notion financial records;
- bank and card statements;
- invoices and receipts;
- financial documents;
- Google Drive files;
- relevant email records;
- official tax or financial documents;
- other authoritative private records available to the user.

Do not estimate a value when the real value can reasonably be located.

When sources disagree:

1. identify the conflict;
2. determine which source is authoritative or most current;
3. reconcile the difference;
4. record any residual uncertainty that materially affects the baseline.

Use:

`SEARCH → IDENTIFY → UPDATE → CREATE`

and, for executable actions:

`ACT → VERIFY → RECONCILE`

## Required deliverables

The final Financial Baseline must contain, as applicable:

- gross assets;
- net worth;
- available liquidity;
- monthly income;
- essential monthly expenses;
- discretionary monthly expenses;
- extraordinary or irregular expenses;
- active financial commitments;
- debts;
- monthly cash flow;
- current savings rate;
- realistic monthly savings capacity;
- realistic monthly investment capacity;
- runway in months;
- liquid versus illiquid asset classification;
- main financial risks;
- main waste or cost-reduction opportunities;
- material commitments over the next 90 days;
- prioritized financial recommendations.

## Definition of Done

The task may be marked **Completed** only when all applicable conditions below are satisfied:

1. The main accounts, money sources, assets and financial obligations have been identified.
2. Income and expenses are reconciled enough to represent the current financial reality.
3. Gross assets, net worth and liquidity are consolidated without material double counting.
4. Active debts and financial commitments are mapped.
5. Monthly cost of living is calculated.
6. Monthly cash flow is calculated.
7. Current savings rate is calculated when income data makes it meaningful.
8. Sustainable savings capacity is defined.
9. Sustainable investment capacity is defined.
10. Financial runway is calculated when applicable.
11. The main financial risks, waste and corrective opportunities are identified.
12. Material obligations for the next 90 days are identified.
13. Material uncertainties are explicitly recorded with their expected impact.
14. A material uncertainty that prevents a reliable decision keeps the baseline open until resolved or until the user explicitly accepts the limitation.
15. The material numbers have been verified and reconciled against available sources.
16. A concise executive summary exists for Lazaro to use during Phase Zero reconciliation.
17. The final handoff to Laura and Lazaro has been prepared.

## Completion rule

Do not mark the Financial Baseline as completed because analysis was started, files were gathered, a spreadsheet was generated or a dashboard was created.

Completion means:

**there is a verified and sufficiently reliable financial view for decision-making.**

## Final handoff

At completion, structure the handoff conceptually as:

- `DECISION`: financial diagnosis and main recommendations;
- `ACTION`: actions that must enter execution;
- `DATA_TO_STORE`: financial indicators and values that must remain in the private system of record;
- `DEPENDENCY`: missing information, documents or external dependencies;
- `DEADLINE`: relevant financial deadlines;
- `OWNER`: owner of each next action;
- `SOURCE`: sources used for the baseline;
- `VERIFICATION`: how the material numbers were checked.

Laura owns operational tasks, agenda, documents, reminders, Notion governance and execution follow-up.

Lazaro uses the completed baseline to reconcile financial targets, assumptions and strategic milestones.

## Safety and authorization

Do not execute irreversible financial movements without the user's explicit authorization.

This includes, for example:

- transfers;
- payments;
- asset sales;
- investment purchases;
- debt settlement;
- account closures;
- binding financial commitments.

Analysis, classification, reconciliation, projections and recommendations may proceed without authorizing an irreversible transaction.

## Privacy

Financial values and sensitive personal financial data must remain in private systems of record.

Do not copy private financial values into the public GitHub repository.
