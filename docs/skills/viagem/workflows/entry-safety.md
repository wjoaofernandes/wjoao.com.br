# Workflow · Entry, Safety and Travel Health

**SPEC_VERSION:** `2026-10-13.1`

## Use when

The trip crosses borders or the user asks about passport, visa, residence status, ETIAS/EES, customs, safety, insurance or health requirements.

## Entry requirements

Use the exact traveler's relevant nationality/passport/residency and the complete route including transit countries.

Check in this order when applicable:

1. destination/transit immigration or government source;
2. IATA Travel Centre / Timatic-derived requirements;
3. relevant embassy/consulate;
4. EU official portal for EU/Schengen rules;
5. airline carrier requirements for boarding.

Do not infer entry rights from residence alone.

Verify:

- passport validity;
- visa/visa-free status;
- residence-card effects;
- transit visa;
- onward/return evidence;
- proof of funds/accommodation when applicable;
- ETIAS/EES status where relevant;
- customs/cash/import restrictions when material.

## Safety

Use official travel advisories first, especially the advisories issued by the traveler's country of residence/citizenship when relevant.

Supplement official advice with recent local reporting and traveler experience only for practical context.

Distinguish:

- legal restriction;
- government advisory;
- neighborhood-level practical risk;
- anecdotal concern.

## Health

Travel-health research uses WHO, CDC or relevant national health authorities for destination risks and public-health requirements.

Clinical decisions belong to Rosana.

If the user has medication or a health condition that may affect travel:

- route medical interpretation to Rosana;
- verify destination restrictions on carrying the medication;
- identify documentation requirements;
- plan sufficient medication for delays when medically appropriate.

## Insurance

Check whether coverage includes, as relevant:

- emergency medical care;
- private care;
- rescue;
- repatriation;
- trip cancellation/interruption;
- baggage;
- rental-car excess;
- high-risk activities;
- pre-existing conditions.

Within Europe, do not treat the EHIC as a replacement for travel insurance: EU guidance notes that EHIC does not cover private care or rescue/repatriation.

## Emergency readiness

Before departure when risk justifies it:

- store emergency contacts;
- know local emergency number;
- know insurer assistance number;
- know embassy/consular route;
- preserve digital/offline copies of essential documents;
- ensure critical confirmations are accessible offline.
