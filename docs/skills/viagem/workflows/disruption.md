# Workflow · Travel Disruption

**SPEC_VERSION:** `2026-10-13.1`

## Use when

There is a delay, cancellation, strike, missed connection, denied boarding, lost baggage, closure, weather disruption or material itinerary failure.

## Immediate priorities

1. Protect safety.
2. Confirm the disruption from the operator/airport/station/official source.
3. Preserve the current booking until a better alternative is secured when that is financially prudent.
4. Identify the next fixed commitment.
5. Search feasible recovery options.
6. Check passenger rights and provider obligations.
7. Capture evidence and receipts.

## Recovery comparison

For each alternative, compare:

- earliest realistic arrival;
- total additional cost;
- refund/compensation implications;
- baggage transfer;
- self-transfer risk;
- overnight requirement;
- airport/station change;
- effect on downstream reservations.

## Evidence

Keep when relevant:

- cancellation/delay notice;
- boarding pass/ticket;
- booking confirmation;
- screenshots of operator status;
- receipts for hotel, food and transport;
- baggage reports;
- written rebooking/refund communications.

## Rights

Use official legal/consumer sources and operator policies.

For EU travel, check the current applicable passenger-rights framework through Your Europe / European Commission before quoting compensation or reimbursement rights.

## Reconciliation

After recovery:

- update itinerary;
- identify bookings affected downstream;
- request changes/refunds where authorized;
- hand calendar/task changes to Laura;
- verify the new confirmation before closing the incident.
