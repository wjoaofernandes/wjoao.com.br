# Workflow · Plan Trip

**SPEC_VERSION:** `2026-10-13.1`

## Use when

The request involves destination selection, transport, accommodation, activities, neighborhoods, itinerary design or total trip comparison.

## Workflow

1. Build or recover the traveler brief.
2. Identify hard constraints and soft preferences.
3. Research destination context and seasonality.
4. Research transport options.
5. Research accommodation areas and representative properties.
6. Research activities, food and local movement.
7. Research weather/climate appropriate to the time horizon.
8. Estimate total trip cost by category.
9. Filter infeasible options.
10. Create a geographically coherent itinerary.
11. Verify critical places, hours and transfers.
12. Stress-test the plan for fatigue, closures and disruption.
13. Present the best plan plus only meaningful alternatives.

## Parallel research model

When tools allow, research in parallel:

- **Destination lane**: neighborhoods, seasonality, major events, local transport, attraction context.
- **Transport lane**: flights/trains/buses/ferries and door-to-door time.
- **Accommodation lane**: location, total price, cancellation, reviews, transit access.
- **Experience lane**: attractions, restaurants, events and reservation requirements.
- **Weather lane**: forecast or seasonal baseline.
- **Budget lane**: transport + stay + local transport + food + activities + fees + buffer.

Synthesize only after the relevant lanes complete.

## Destination selection

When destination is open-ended, score candidates against the user's actual goal. Typical factors:

- total trip cost;
- travel time from origin;
- weather/season;
- crowd level;
- safety and entry friction;
- cultural/food/experience fit;
- language friction;
- transport convenience;
- trip length efficiency.

Avoid generic "top destinations" lists when the user's constraints allow a narrower decision.

## Transport

Compare door-to-door, not airport-to-airport only.

Include when relevant:

- trip to departure point;
- check-in/security buffer;
- connection time;
- baggage/seat fees;
- arrival transfer;
- time-zone effect;
- overnight arrival cost;
- risk of separate tickets/self-transfer.

For rail/bus/ferry, prefer operator timetables for the final choice.

## Accommodation

Evaluate:

- final price;
- cancellation;
- neighborhood;
- travel time to itinerary anchors;
- late arrival/check-in constraints;
- noise;
- air conditioning/heating when seasonal;
- Wi‑Fi/workspace when relevant;
- parking when driving;
- accessibility requirements;
- review recency and recurring complaints.

Do not recommend a cheaper room if its location creates greater total transport/time cost without stating that trade-off.

## Daily itinerary design

- Cluster activities geographically.
- Keep arrival/departure days lighter.
- Add realistic meal, rest and transit time.
- Check days of closure and timed-entry requirements.
- Avoid excessive backtracking.
- Use reservations as anchors.
- Put optional items in a backup/ideas bucket instead of overcrowding the main schedule.
- Prefer one excellent plan over many weak alternatives.

## Budget structure

When requested, break out:

- long-distance transport;
- accommodation;
- local transport;
- food;
- activities/tickets;
- baggage/seat/booking fees;
- insurance;
- connectivity;
- taxes/city fees;
- contingency buffer.

Separate observed prices from estimates.
