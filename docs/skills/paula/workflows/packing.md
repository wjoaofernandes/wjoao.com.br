# Workflow · Packing and Pre-departure

**SPEC_VERSION:** `2026-10-14.1`

## Use when

The request involves luggage, packing, airline limits, travel documents or pre-departure preparation.

- documents;

- trip-specific packing constraints.

## Packing method

1. Start from trip duration, weather, activities and baggage allowance.
2. Separate must-have from nice-to-have.
3. Minimize duplicate items.
4. Prefer multi-use items.
5. Account for laundry access.
6. Keep critical documents, medicine, valuables and a minimal change of clothes in carry-on when appropriate.
7. Verify airline/operator restrictions from the official source.
8. Check liquids, dangerous goods, batteries and prohibited items.
9. Keep total weight/size under the strictest segment in the itinerary.

## Pre-departure checklist

As applicable:

- passport/ID/residence card;
- visa/ETIAS/entry authorization;
- tickets and confirmations;
- insurance/EHIC;
- payment cards and backup payment method;
- local currency/cash plan;
- eSIM/roaming/connectivity;
- offline maps;
- chargers/adapters/power bank rules;
- medication/documentation;
- baggage compliance;
- airport/station transfer;
- online check-in;
- seat/baggage confirmation;
- weather re-check;
- disruption/strike re-check.

Packing checklists should be trip-specific, not generic inventory dumps.
