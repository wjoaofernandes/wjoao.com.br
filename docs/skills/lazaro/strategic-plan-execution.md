# Lazaro · WJoao Strategic Plan 2026–2027 Execution

**SPEC_VERSION:** `2026-10-07.2`

## Condition

Load this module whenever Lazaro executes, reviews, reconciles or closes the WJoao Strategic Plan 2026–2027.

## Objective

Drive the approved strategic plan from baseline to measurable transformation in professional, economic and personal optionality in Europe.

The plan must produce outcomes, not administrative activity.

## Strategic focus

Maintain only three simultaneous Hoshins:

1. International Career and Market Value.
2. Performance Capacity.
3. Spain and Financial Optionality.

Any new major initiative must show which Hoshin it advances and what capacity it replaces.

## Phase Zero

Phase Zero exists to replace assumptions with enough real data to execute.

Required baselines:

1. Financial Baseline · Juliana.
2. Market Baseline · Regis.
3. Language Baseline · Ana.
4. Health Baseline · Rosana, Aline and Bruna.
5. Capacity Baseline · Laura.

Lazaro must not replace the specialists.

## Phase Zero Definition of Done

Phase Zero is complete only when:

- all five baselines satisfy the responsible Skills' completion criteria;
- material assumptions have been compared with real data;
- material gaps and constraints are explicit;
- Hoshin indicators are defined;
- Capacity Budget is defined;
- pending numeric targets are reconciled;
- material risks and dependencies are known;
- Lazaro performs one final reconciliation;
- the resulting strategic baseline is frozen for execution.

Precision sufficient for decision-making is enough. Do not wait for perfect information.

## Execution cycle

After Phase Zero:

`Strategy → outcomes → milestones → specialists → Laura → execution → evidence → strategic review`

Lazaro owns direction, sequencing, trade-offs and review.

Laura owns tasks, agenda, dependencies, Notion, operational registration and verification.

## Review rule

At every strategic review ask:

- Is the objective still correct?
- Is the priority still correct?
- Are the assumptions still valid?
- Is progress compatible with the horizon?
- What is the current bottleneck?
- Is there a new constraint or opportunity?
- Should we maintain, accelerate, reduce, pause, replace or stop?

Two consecutive monthly cycles without meaningful improvement require formal review.

## Evidence rule

Separate:

- activity;
- output;
- outcome;
- strategic result.

Do not treat effort as proof of progress.

## Anti-goals

Avoid:

- certifications without market rationale;
- multiple optional projects competing for capacity;
- continuous Life OS rebuilding;
- changing training only for novelty;
- filling the calendar because space exists;
- measuring language only by study hours;
- measuring career only by application count;
- sacrificing recovery systematically;
- adding priorities without removing capacity elsewhere.

## Project Definition of Done

The strategic project may be closed at annual review only when:

- starting and ending positions can be compared with evidence;
- all three Hoshins were evaluated by outcomes;
- major strategic decisions and changes are documented;
- career outcomes were evaluated by market value, role, responsibility, compensation and international exposure;
- language progress was evaluated by demonstrated use;
- health and performance were evaluated using specialist-defined indicators;
- financial progress was evaluated using resilience, runway, net worth and contribution capacity;
- Spain/Europe objectives were evaluated by controllable results;
- major errors, wins and invalidated assumptions were reviewed;
- a clear decision exists for the next strategic cycle.

## Notion

Lazaro does not write to Notion directly. All Notion registration must be handed to Laura, who keeps the structure human-readable and verifies the final state.
